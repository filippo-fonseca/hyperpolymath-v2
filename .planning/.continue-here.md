   on `isSuccess` (`dailyReady`) instead of `isFetched`.

   wrote both `jarvis_turns` rows as fire-and-forget `void` promises; on Vercel the
   function freezes at stream close, dropping the writes.
   Fix: capture both inserts (`userTurnPersist` + `assistantPersist`) and
   `await Promise.allSettled([...])` before `controller.close()`.
