   - Two number inputs:
     * Actual duration (min) — pre-filled with activity.plannedDurationMin || ''

   - Close (X) cancels without writing.
   - The dialog must satisfy D-08: opening + immediately pressing Enter (no input touched) calls completeActivity with the pre-filled (= planned) values. To make Enter submit even when focus is the primary button (default browser behavior), wrap the inputs and buttons in a form element with onSubmit calling the primary handler and preventing default. autoFocus on the primary button.
