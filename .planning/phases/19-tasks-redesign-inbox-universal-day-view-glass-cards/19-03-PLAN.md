<objective>
Restyle the task cards and the detail panel to the settings-page glass language, and add the inline clear-date affordance. Two locked decisions: D-09 (glassier panel + cards matching `glass-tile` / `--glass-*`) and D-03 (inline "clear" directly on the due-date field that moves a task to the Inbox).

Purpose: The user dislikes the heavy flat panel and wants the clean, glassy settings-menu feel; and clearing a date should be a one-click affordance on the field itself, not buried in a menu. This plan touches only two files (TaskCard, TaskDetailPanel) so it runs in parallel with the JARVIS plan and ahead of the TasksClient refactors.

Output: `TaskCard` uses `glass-tile` with selected/lesno treatments; `TaskDetailPanel` has a glassy Sheet interior, a glass Save button, and an inline X clear affordance on the due-date field.
</objective>
