Lucide's 2px default applies to those project icons until a future polish plan wires through the prop. Lucide icons used directly (`Folder`, `Plus`, `MoreHorizontal`, `ChevronLeft`/`Right`, `XIcon`, `Calendar`, `AlertCircle`) all receive `strokeWidth={1.5}` per UI-SPEC §8a.

- **Commit:** `d513b73`
