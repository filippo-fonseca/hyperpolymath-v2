<objective>
When a JARVIS command is fired via Cmd+K (which only opens OFF /today) or by voice on a non-/today route, it currently runs `streamJarvis` with toast-only receipts and never writes to `jarvis_turns`. As a result the turn never becomes part of the JARVIS conversation, and the /today console (which renders from SSR `initialTurns` + its own local `handleSubmit` only) never sees it — live or on reload.

This plan makes those turns (a) persist to `jarvis_turns` and (b) appear as normal conversation receipts on /today in real-time and on reload, while keeping the existing toast/TTS/FSM UX byte-for-byte unchanged.

Purpose: Cmd+K is a primary JARVIS entry point; today it silently drops out of the conversation record. JARVIS is THE core surface of the app — its conversation log must be complete and live.
Output: GlobalJarvisHandler persists turns; JarvisConsole live-merges externally-created turns into its local scrollback (deduped by id).
</objective>
