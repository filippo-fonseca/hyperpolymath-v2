# Phase 3 — The Makerspace: Vision Notes (Filippo's own words)

> Captured live at session kickoff (2026-07-06). Source of truth for the Fable planner's
> creative brief. These are the user's direct words + intent — do not sanitize away the vibe.

## The core vision
- The current **calendar view (Phase-2 Meridian Ring) is "a bit shit."** It stays only if it
  earns a place as ONE widget among many — not as the whole sky.
- What he actually wants for the **whole 3D space**: **"Tony Stark's live makerspace."** A
  first-person workspace he steps into.
- The LifeOS surfaces that today live as **2D browser pages become holographic widgets** floating
  in that space — "the widgets of what was LifeOS on the normal browser."
- **Swipeable**: widgets he can **swipe in and out of view**.
- **Navigable in 3D**: "a space where I can come in and in 3D navigate my widgets and
  everything, move them around, and interact with them."
- **Move them around**: the widgets are repositionable in space, not fixed furniture — he
  arranges his own workbench.
- **Interact with them**: full interaction, not just display (the TodayPanel "complete a task
  from the world" pattern generalized to every widget).
- **See the tree structure of areas & projects**: "perhaps see the tree structure of my areas
  and projects" — the areas/projects tree is a first-class thing he wants visible/navigable in
  the space. (Note: he has repeatedly said the areas tree is the ONE part of the old design he
  loves — carry that forward.)

## Hard "don't"s from him
- **Do NOT start from scratch code-wise.** Huge amount of gruntwork already done; keep the
  technical foundation + the 3D world + Phase-1 systems.
- **Phase 1 was good and already has a working widget** (the Today panel) — build on from there;
  it's the template.

## Standing gates / doctrine (unchanged)
- Fable plans the new direction; Opus executes; Sonnet docs; Haiku trivial. Fable never writes code.
- Human gate BEFORE any code-writing agents fan out: Filippo reviews & blesses the Fable plan first.
- One truth, two theatres: widgets reuse the exact 2D data hooks / server actions / query keys.
